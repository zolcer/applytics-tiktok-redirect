Allympo

A personal analytics app for iPhone and iPad that shows statistics of your own accounts in one place — App Store Connect, Google Analytics, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X.

With TikTok, Allympo shows your profile's follower count and the views, likes, comments and shares of your own videos. It only reads data — it never posts or changes anything on your account.
