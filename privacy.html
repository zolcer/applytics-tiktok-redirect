Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Allympo is an analytics app for iPhone and iPad that shows statistics of accounts you connect to it. This policy explains what data the app accesses and what happens with it.

No servers, no collection

Allympo has no backend. The app talks directly to the platforms you sign in to (TikTok, X, Meta, Google, Apple App Store Connect) from your device. We, the developer, never receive, see or store any of your data.

Data the app accesses

Only after you sign in to an account, and only for that account:

  • TikTok: your basic profile (display name, username, avatar), follower count, and the list of your own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.
  • X: your profile, follower count, and your own posts with their public metrics.
  • Facebook and Instagram: Pages, Instagram accounts and ad accounts you manage, their Insights, posts and ad campaign statistics.
  • Google Analytics and App Store Connect: analytics of properties and apps you have access to.

The app only reads data. It never posts, edits or deletes anything on your accounts.

Where data is stored

  • Access tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device.
  • API responses are cached on your device for up to 24 hours so screens load faster.
  • For TikTok and X, the daily follower count is recorded on your device to show follower growth over time.

Nothing is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising or tracking.

Deleting your data

Signing out of an account in the app's Settings removes its access token and clears cached responses. Deleting the app removes all its data from your device. You can also revoke the app's access at any time in your TikTok, X, Facebook or Google account settings.
